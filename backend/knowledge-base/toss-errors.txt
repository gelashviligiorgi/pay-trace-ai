SOURCE: Toss Payments

CODE: INVALID_CARD
MEANING: Card number is invalid or not supported
CAUSE: Card number format is incorrect, fails Luhn check, or the card type is not supported by Toss Payments
RETRYABLE: no
FIX: Ask customer to verify card number or use a different card
SYNONYMS: bad card number, wrong card number, card not accepted, unsupported card, card format invalid
CONTEXT: Triggered when the card number entered does not pass basic validation or belongs to a card network that Toss does not support. Customer should double-check the card number for typos or try a different card.

CODE: CARD_EXPIRED
MEANING: The card used for payment has expired
CAUSE: Card expiration date has passed and the card is no longer valid
RETRYABLE: no
FIX: Request updated card information from customer
SYNONYMS: card is old, outdated card, expired payment method, card out of date, past expiry date, card no longer valid
CONTEXT: Customer is attempting to pay with a card whose expiration month and year have passed. They likely need to update their saved payment method with a newly issued replacement card. Common when customers have not updated stored card details after receiving a renewed card from their bank.

CODE: PAYMENT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED
MEANING: Payment limit exceeded for this account or card
CAUSE: Transaction exceeds the daily or monthly payment limits set by Toss or the issuing bank
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Customer should contact Toss support or try a smaller transaction amount
SYNONYMS: account limit reached, too many transactions, spending limit hit, over limit, limit exceeded, maximum payment exceeded, transaction cap
CONTEXT: Toss Payments enforces per-transaction and cumulative daily or monthly limits. This may also be enforced by the customer's issuing bank independently. Customer can split the payment into smaller amounts or contact Toss support to request a limit increase.

CODE: AUTHENTICATION_FAILED
MEANING: Customer identity authentication failed during payment
CAUSE: Failed to verify customer identity through the authentication process. The customer did not complete or passed incorrect credentials during identity verification.
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Ask customer to retry authentication process with correct credentials
SYNONYMS: identity verification failed, identity check failed, verification failed, failed to verify identity, authentication error, could not verify customer, identity not confirmed
CONTEXT: Toss requires identity verification as part of the payment flow, particularly for first-time users or high-value transactions. Failure can mean the customer entered wrong credentials, the verification session timed out, or the identity check could not be completed. This is equivalent to identity verification failure in the Toss payment context.

CODE: PROVIDER_ERROR
MEANING: Technical error on the payment provider side
CAUSE: Internal technical issue within Toss Payments infrastructure or upstream payment provider
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Retry transaction after a short delay; escalate if persistent
SYNONYMS: system error, provider down, technical error, payment system error, infrastructure error, backend error, toss system error
CONTEXT: An unexpected technical failure occurred on the Toss Payments side rather than being caused by the customer or their card. These errors are typically transient. Retry after a few seconds. If the error persists across multiple retries it may indicate a broader Toss outage — check Toss status page.
