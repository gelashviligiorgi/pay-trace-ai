SOURCE: PayPal

CODE: INSTRUMENT_DECLINED
MEANING: Payment instrument was declined by the processor
CAUSE: Issuing bank declined the transaction at the card network level
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Customer should contact their bank or use an alternative payment method
SYNONYMS: payment method declined, card rejected, funding source declined, payment instrument rejected
CONTEXT: The underlying funding source attached to the PayPal account was rejected by the issuing bank. This could be a linked debit card, credit card, or bank account. The decline originates at the bank level, not PayPal. Customer should check with their bank or switch to PayPal balance if available.

CODE: INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS
MEANING: Account does not have sufficient balance to cover the transaction
CAUSE: PayPal balance or linked funding source lacks the required funds
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Ask customer to add funds or link a different payment source
SYNONYMS: not enough money, low balance, no funds, account empty, balance too low, insufficient balance, cant pay, not enough in account
CONTEXT: The customer's PayPal balance is too low and no backup funding source is available or their linked bank account or card also has insufficient funds. Common with PayPal balance-only accounts. Customer needs to top up their PayPal account or add a funded card.

CODE: TRANSACTION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED
MEANING: Transaction exceeds the account's spending limits
CAUSE: PayPal account has spending limits that were exceeded by this transaction
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Customer needs to verify their PayPal account or wait until the limit resets
SYNONYMS: account limit reached, spending limit exceeded, over limit, too large transaction, amount too high, limit hit, exceeded maximum
CONTEXT: Unverified PayPal accounts have lower transaction limits. Customer may need to complete PayPal's verification process by linking a bank account or confirming identity to raise their limits. Also applies to single transaction caps.

CODE: FRAUD_DETECTED
MEANING: Transaction flagged as potentially fraudulent by PayPal's risk systems
CAUSE: PayPal's fraud detection system identified suspicious activity based on unusual patterns, mismatched billing details, velocity checks, or known fraud indicators
RETRYABLE: no
FIX: Customer must contact PayPal support to resolve security concerns
SYNONYMS: fraud suspected, suspicious transaction, flagged as fraud, potential fraud, fraudulent activity, security block, transaction blocked for fraud, looks like fraud
CONTEXT: PayPal's automated risk engine blocked the transaction. Triggers include mismatched shipping and billing addresses, multiple failed attempts, unusual spending patterns, or the account being flagged previously. Customer must contact PayPal directly — this cannot be resolved by retrying.

CODE: REDIRECT_REQUIRED
MEANING: Customer needs to complete additional authentication before payment can proceed
CAUSE: PayPal requires additional verification or security step to authorize this transaction
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Ensure customer completes the redirect flow to authenticate
SYNONYMS: authentication required, verification needed, complete verification, additional step required, redirect to PayPal
CONTEXT: PayPal needs the customer to complete an extra authentication step, typically through a browser redirect to PayPal's hosted page. Common in server-side integrations where the redirect was not properly handled. The transaction will complete once the customer finishes the verification flow.
