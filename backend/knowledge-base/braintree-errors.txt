CODE: 2001
MEANING: Insufficient Funds
CAUSE: Customer's account does not have enough funds to complete the transaction
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Ask customer to use a different payment method or add funds to their account
SYNONYMS: not enough money, low balance, insufficient balance, no funds, account empty, cant afford, balance too low
CONTEXT: Common during end of month when customers run low on funds. Also triggered when a customer tries to pay with a prepaid card that has insufficient balance. The transaction amount exceeds what is currently available in the linked bank account or card.

CODE: 2002
MEANING: Limit Exceeded
CAUSE: Transaction amount exceeds card's daily or transaction limit set by the issuing bank
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Customer should contact their bank to increase limits or use an alternative payment method
SYNONYMS: account limit reached, spending limit exceeded, daily limit hit, transaction cap reached, over limit, maximum amount exceeded
CONTEXT: Triggered when a single transaction or cumulative daily spending surpasses the bank-imposed limit. Common with debit cards that have strict daily caps. Customer may not be aware of their limit until it is hit.

CODE: 2003
MEANING: Cardholder's Activity Limit Exceeded
CAUSE: Too many transactions attempted in a short period of time
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Wait and retry later, or ask customer to contact their bank
SYNONYMS: too many transactions, too many attempts, velocity limit, frequency limit, activity limit, transaction count exceeded
CONTEXT: Triggered by excessive payment attempts within a short window. Banks impose velocity limits to prevent fraud. Can happen if a customer retries a failed payment too many times in quick succession.

CODE: 2004
MEANING: Expired Card
CAUSE: The card's expiration date has passed
RETRYABLE: no
FIX: Request updated card information from customer
SYNONYMS: card is old, outdated card, card out of date, card no longer valid, past expiry, expired payment method
CONTEXT: Customer is attempting to pay with a card whose expiration month and year have passed. Often happens when a new card was issued but the customer has not updated their saved payment details. Card networks reject expired cards at authorization stage.

CODE: 2010
MEANING: Card Issuer Declined CVV
CAUSE: CVV verification failed at the issuing bank
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Ask customer to verify and re-enter the correct CVV code
SYNONYMS: wrong CVV, incorrect security code, bad CVV, wrong CVC, security number mismatch, wrong card verification value
CONTEXT: Customer entered an incorrect 3-digit security code from the back of their card. Also triggered if the CVV on file does not match what the issuing bank has recorded. Some virtual cards have dynamically generated CVVs that change periodically.

CODE: 2038
MEANING: Processor Declined
CAUSE: Generic decline from the card processor without a specific reason provided
RETRYABLE: yes
FIX: Suggest customer contact their bank or try a different payment method
SYNONYMS: payment rejected, transaction declined, card declined, processor rejection, generic decline, unspecified decline
CONTEXT: Catch-all decline code when the processor does not return a specific reason. The bank has declined the transaction but has not communicated why. Could be due to fraud suspicion, unusual activity, or internal bank policy. Customer should call their bank for details.

CODE: 2046
MEANING: Declined - Call Issuer
CAUSE: Bank requires cardholder to call for manual authorization before the transaction can proceed
RETRYABLE: no
FIX: Customer must contact their bank before retrying the transaction
SYNONYMS: need to call bank, contact bank, call your bank, bank requires contact, manual authorization required, issuer contact required
CONTEXT: Bank has flagged this transaction and requires the cardholder to call the number on the back of their card to verify identity or authorize the payment. Common after a fraud alert is triggered or when the bank detects unusual spending patterns.

CODE: 2074
MEANING: Duplicate Transaction
CAUSE: Same transaction submitted multiple times within a short timeframe
RETRYABLE: no
FIX: Check if original transaction succeeded; wait before attempting again
SYNONYMS: duplicate payment, repeated transaction, double charge, same transaction twice, already processed
CONTEXT: Triggered when an identical transaction with the same amount, card, and merchant is submitted too quickly after a previous attempt. Often caused by a user double-clicking a payment button or a retry loop in the backend. Always check if the original succeeded before retrying.
